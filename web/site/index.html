Built for chaos

Newtonian Physics

Real newtonian physics. Combine space friction with thrust and momentum. Every moment you make could result in your death.

An Assortment of Asteroids

Face the challenge of unique asteroids every level. Reflective, teleporting, invisible, quantum, tough, armoured and phasing. Every level poses new challenges.

Numerous Weapons

Mines, giga-mines, homing missiles, shields, and God Mode invincibility. Stack nova charges on your kill streak, and wipe the whole screen of enemies.

Gravity hazards

Black holes warp space and swallow anything that drifts too close. From level 10, gravity wells make killing even simple asteroids a challenge.

Enemy stations

Late-game stations deploy waves of hunting ships with escalating AI. No pilots have ever been this far. Prove your worth by completing more than 20 levels.

Two Player Split-Screen

Split screen for two pilots. Toggle friendly fire for maximum carnage. Bring a second keyboard or controller and prove your skill to your friends.
