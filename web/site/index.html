A fast, physics-driven top-down space shooter.

Newtonian thrust. Wrapping space. Asteroids that test your every move. Survive endless generations — solo or two-player.

Built for chaos

True momentum

Real Newtonian flight — you carry your velocity. Master the thrust and your trajectory, because space won't slow you down.

Asteroids with attitude

Reflective, teleporting, invisible, quantum, tough, armoured, phasing. Each level unlocks deadlier rock.

An arsenal

Mines, giga-mines, homing missiles, shields, and God Mode invincibility. Stack Nova charges and detonate the whole field.

Gravity hazards

Black holes warp space and swallow anything that drifts too close. From level 10, the centre is no longer safe.

Enemy stations

Late-game stations deploy waves of hunting ships with escalating AI. The galaxy stops being empty.

Two-player co-op

Split-screen for two pilots. Friendly fire optional — bring a second keyboard and find out who your friends are.
